DNS Recursive Resolution Evidence
=================================

Assessment Type:
Black-box network security assessment

Target:
TARGET_IP

Test:
Recursive DNS resolution for external domains

Command:
dig @TARGET_IP google.com A

Observed:
status: NOERROR
flags: qr rd ra

External domains successfully resolved:

- google.com
- microsoft.com
- cloudflare.com
- wikipedia.org
- example.com

Interpretation:
The resolver successfully processed DNS queries for external
Internet domains and advertised recursive DNS availability
through the RA flag.

Conclusion:
The DNS service demonstrated Internet-accessible recursive
resolution functionality.

Evidence Classification:
Confirmed
